# 01 — Sensing, Connectivity & Networking

Foundational concepts behind how industrial systems sense, communicate and exchange information.

This domain explores the technologies that make machines, devices, processes and industrial assets observable, connected and digitally accessible.

## Why This Matters

Connectivity is the foundation of Industry 4.0.

Without reliable sensing, communication and networking, higher-level capabilities such as analytics, traceability, AI, smart manufacturing and autonomous decision-making cannot exist.
