# Case Studies

Real industrial engagements and field observations.

Each study follows a consistent pattern:
- **Situation →** What was happening?
- **Observation →** What was seen on the floor?
- **Root cause →** Why was it happening?
- **Proposed solution →** What was recommended?
- **Impact →** What changed (or could change)?
- **Insight →** What does this teach about the practice?

These are not academic cases.
They are structured field notes from manufacturing environments.

## Adding a New Case

Use this structure:

**Situation** — Context, customer, environment.

**Observation** — What was seen during Gemba walk or assessment?

**Root Cause** — Why was it happening? Where did visibility break?

**Proposed Solution** — What was recommended?

**Expected Impact** — What would change?

**Insight** — One sentence takeaway.

**Related Topics** — Links to domains and glossary terms.
